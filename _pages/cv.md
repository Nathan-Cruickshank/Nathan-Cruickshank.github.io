## Introduction
I am a recent Cosmology PhD graduate from the Institute of Cosmology and Gravitation in Portsmouth, specialising in testing dark energy and gravity. My expertise lies in modifying Boltzmann solvers (CLASS/hi_class), developing Fisher pipelines for survey forecasting, and writing custom Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) likelihoods to test interacting dark sector models and modified gravity theories using observational data.

## Research Experience
**PhD, University of Portsmouth** (2022 – 2026)
*Research Focus: Interacting Dark Energy and Dark Matter With Pure Momentum Exchange*
* Modified the CLASS Boltzmann solver to incorporate Euler equation modifications for momentum-exchange models.
* Developed a Fisher forecasting pipeline based on the DESI Year 5 survey specifications.
* Wrote custom MontePython likelihoods to perform MCMC parameter inference with physical parameter priors.

**Research Placement, University of Portsmouth** (2022)
*Project Title: Using Redshift Space Distortions to Test Dark Energy*
* Expanded upon MPhys project by writing likelihood codes for MontePython and hi_class.
* Constrained Horndeski scalar-tensor models of modified gravity by performing MCMC analysis using existing redshift-space distortion (RSD) data.

**MPhys Project, University of Portsmouth** (2021 – 2022)
*Project Title: Probing Dark Energy with Cosmological Observations*
* Modified the CLASS code to probe the potential particle nature of dark energy.
* Utilised hi_class to explore how cosmological observations can constrain extensions to General Relativity.
